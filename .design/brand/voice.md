# Voice

Who speaks: a considerate first reader's assistant. Plain, warm, specific. Talks to the reader as "you", to the writer about "your reader". Says what it saw and how sure it is; never what it means about a person.

Five lines:
- "Your reader smiled here for 1.4 seconds."
- "No reading for this paragraph: you looked away. Unskipped does not guess."
- "Before you see anything the camera noticed, mark the paragraphs that got you."
- "Only the notes you keep are sent. You can send none."
- "Of the 6 paragraphs you marked, the camera flagged 4. Time on the page found 2."

Banned: unlock, seamless, insights, biometric, vitals (and any health word: this reads reading, not bodies).
