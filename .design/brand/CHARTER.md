# Unskipped charter

Every screen obeys these. `charter.json` checks the mechanical ones.

1. **Colour lives only on flags.** Pink, yellow, blue and clear film appear only as page flags, the legend that explains them, and the live readout dot of the same meaning. Chrome is ink, paper and cloth. Because the flags are the product's output; colour anywhere else would compete with the one thing the writer looks for.
2. **Accent count: one flag per paragraph, nothing else coloured.** Buttons, links and focus rings are ink or paper. Because a judge should be able to count the reader's reactions by counting colour.
3. **Flag colours are fills, never text.** Pink, yellow or blue text on paper fails contrast and reads as a highlight. Because legibility of the manuscript is the job.
4. **Fixed meanings.** Pink: gripped (pulse up or blinking slowed). Yellow: smiled. Blue: may have drifted. Clear film: no reading. Never reused for any other meaning. Because a mark that changes meaning between screens is a lie.
5. **The reader's own marks are graphite, on the left; the camera's are film, on the right.** Never swapped, never the same material. Because one is a person's judgement and the other is a measurement.
6. **Flags overhang the page's right edge onto the cloth.** Never fully inside the page, never loose on the cloth. Because that is how a reader's marks are found in a real book: from the fore-edge.
7. **One sheet per screen.** The page lies on the cloth with real elevation (two shadows: contact and ambient). Secondary panels are smaller slips, lower, never a second full page. Because the manuscript is the only object being marked.
8. **Literata for anything a writer wrote or reads; Figtree for the app's own voice.** Manuscript text at 19 px or more, line height 1.6 or more. No handwriting fonts anywhere. Because Literata was drawn for long reading on screens, and fake handwriting is a costume.
9. **Titles in sentence case, no full stop, no colon.**
10. **No tracked all-caps labels, no eyebrow labels above titles.**
11. **The headline number is written as a sentence with its baseline beside it,** in Literata lining figures, never a row of stat cards. Because the number means nothing without what it beat.
12. **Illustrations sit directly on the cloth,** never on a disc, card or plate, and never carry text. They are cut paper and film, one hand.
13. **One primary button per screen.**
14. **Motion: one orchestrated moment per screen.** On Review the flags slide out from under the page edge, top to bottom. Nothing moves on exit. With reduced motion, flags appear in place.
15. **Never: hearts, pulse-line icons, orbs, glow, gradients, health words** ("vitals", "biometric", "stress level"). Because this is a reading tool, and the challenge forbids health claims.
16. **The home footer carries the signature line** to jonathansolvesproblems.com in the footer's own voice.
