# Demo script (target 75 to 90 seconds)

1. (0:00) Claire's inbox: three beta reader replies to chapter 7. "Loved it!" "Great chapter!" "Really enjoyed this one." Voice: "Every beta reader says they loved it. None of them says where they stopped reading."
2. (0:08) Elmore Leonard's rule on screen, cited: "Try to leave out the part that readers tend to skip." Voice: "So which part was it?"
3. (0:14) Claire drops chapter 7 (.docx) into Unskipped and sends the reader a link to the session file.
4. (0:20) The reader's side: the consent screen, in plain words. What the camera reads, that video never leaves the laptop, telemetry off, and that they will see every note before anything is sent. They press Start.
5. (0:30) Reading, split screen: the page on the left, the live SmartSpectra readings on the right (pulse, blinks, smile), with the paragraph under the reading line highlighted. A smile on a funny line leaves a yellow flag. Blinking slows in the tense scene: pink.
6. (0:45) The reader looks away at their phone. The paragraph gets a clear film flag: "No reading: you looked away." Voice: "When it cannot tell, it says so instead of guessing."
7. (0:52) Reader finishes, marks their own moments first (before seeing anything), then sees the review screen: every note, each one deletable. They delete one and press Send.
8. (1:02) Result card: of the moments the reader marked, the camera found X; time-per-page found Z.
9. (1:10) FINAL SHOT: Claire opens chapter 7 in Google Docs. The margin is full of comments from the reader's face and pulse; she scrolls to the paragraph marked "drifted: blinking up, pulse back to baseline for 40 seconds" and highlights it to cut.
