I built a small open-source app called Unskipped and I'm looking for one writer from the QWF community who'd like to try it on a chapter.

A beta reader reads the chapter on their own laptop with the camera on. It picks up their pulse, blinks and smiles paragraph by paragraph, and the writer gets back a Word comment on each paragraph where the reader was gripped or may have drifted. The camera processing stays on the reader's laptop, no video leaves it, and the reader approves every note before anything is sent.
